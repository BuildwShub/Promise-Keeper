# Promise Keeper: promo video narration

Audience: company employees first, then managers, freelancers and creators. Tone: calm, warm, Google-product-launch style.
Every claim is true of the product as built. The Outlook scene is a stated vision, not a shipped feature. The example people and company are fictional.

| # | Scene | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hook | Last Tuesday, you wrote five words: "I'll send it by Friday." It's Friday now. And that sentence is buried under two hundred emails. |
| 2 | The problem | Your to-do list only knows what you type into it. But your real commitments live in your inbox: the ones you make, the ones people ask of you, and the ones others owe you. And nothing reads your Sent folder. Until now. |
| 3 | Introducing | Meet Promise Keeper. It lives inside Gmail, reads both sides of your conversations, and quietly finds every commitment. |
| 4a | Use case: spot | Meet Maya, a product manager. She replies to her finance lead: "I'll send the revised budget by Friday." Promise Keeper spots the promise, works out the date, and shows the exact sentence it came from. |
| 4b | Use case: accept | One tap on Accept, and it lands in Google Tasks. Deadlines go on her calendar too. |
| 4c | Use case: requests | Then her manager asks her to review a deck by end of day. And a supplier says they'll ship an order on Monday. Both appear instantly, labelled: what she owes, and what she's waiting on. |
| 4d | Use case: digest | Every morning, one digest shows what's overdue, what's due today, and who owes her. |
| 4e | Use case: nudge | When the supplier goes quiet, one tap writes a polite follow-up draft. She reviews it. Promise Keeper never sends anything for her. |
| 5 | Trust | And it has to be trustworthy. Every item quotes the sentence it came from. Nothing is added until you accept it. We tested it on one hundred and six real emails. Ninety-seven had nothing to find, and it raised zero false alarms. |
| 6 | Who it is for | For employees, nothing slips between meetings. For managers, a clear picture of what is owed. For freelancers, every client promise tracked and every invoice chased. For creators, every sponsor deliverable and deadline, handled. |
| 7 | Outlook vision | Today, it works in Gmail. The same idea can come to Outlook, so every inbox, at work or at home, becomes a commitment tracker. Fewer follow-ups. Less searching. No more "sorry, I forgot." |
| 8 | Close | Promise Keeper. Never lose a promise in your inbox. |
