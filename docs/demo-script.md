# 90-second demo: recording script

A real screen recording beats the walkthrough GIF in the README. This script keeps it to about 90 seconds and shows only things that work.

## Before you record (5 minutes)
1. Use a **clean browser window** (no other tabs, bookmarks bar hidden) and zoom Gmail to 100%.
2. Sign in to Gmail as the demo account. Make sure the **Promise Keeper** side-panel icon is visible.
3. Prepare one **fresh test email** to send during the recording (sent from your second account), for example:
   - Subject: `Q4 plan`, body: `Hi, I'll send the revised Q4 plan by Friday. Thanks.`
4. Open these tabs in order, so you only switch, never search: Gmail inbox, the web dashboard (`/items`), the eval page (`/admin/eval`).
5. Remove anything private from view: other inbox subjects, personal emails in the left column. Blur or crop in the editor, or use a demo account.
6. Recorder: **Windows key + G** (Xbox Game Bar), then the **Record** button. Or the Snipping Tool's screen recorder (Windows key + Shift + R). Record the window, not the whole screen.

## The script
| Time | On screen | Say (one sentence each) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:10 | Gmail inbox | "Every promise I make in email, and every one made to me, ends up buried. Promise Keeper finds them." |
| 0:10 to 0:25 | Send the test email from the second account, switch back, open it | "This arrived a moment ago. Gmail's side panel already shows the commitment it found, due Friday." |
| 0:25 to 0:40 | Point at the quoted sentence in the panel | "It shows the exact sentence it came from, so I can trust it at a glance. Nothing is added until I accept." |
| 0:40 to 0:55 | Click **Accept**, open Google Tasks in the side bar | "Accepting creates a Google Task, and deadlines also become calendar events." |
| 0:55 to 1:05 | Click **Draft follow-up** on a "waiting on" item, open Drafts | "If someone owes me something, one tap writes a follow-up draft. It never sends for me." |
| 1:05 to 1:15 | Open the morning digest email | "Every morning a digest shows what is overdue, due today, and who I am waiting on." |
| 1:15 to 1:30 | Switch to `/admin/eval`, show the runs table | "I measure it. On 106 real emails it raised zero false alarms, with 100 percent precision and 78 percent recall. The first prompt failed my own gate, and the table shows the fix." |

## After recording
- Trim the start and end, keep it under 2 minutes, export as MP4.
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**, or drag the MP4 into a GitHub issue or README edit box (GitHub hosts files up to 10 MB and gives you a link). Paste the link under the GIF in the README.
- Keep the GIF as the fallback: it plays inline without a click.
